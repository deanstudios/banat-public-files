# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: November 3, 2026**

Welcome to **Banat** ("we," "our," or "the App"). Your privacy is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy explains how Banat handles your personal information, health metrics, and progress check-in photos.

---

## 1. Local Storage, Backups and Sharing

Banat is designed as an **offline-first application**. Your profile and health logs are stored in a local Room database, and photos are stored in app-specific files. Banat does not add database encryption; storage protection depends on Android and your device security settings. Android backup and device transfer may copy app data according to your device settings.

- **No Remote Servers**: We do not maintain external servers to collect, upload, or process your personal health metrics or progress photos.
- **User-directed sharing and system backups**: If you share a PDF or photo, the receiving app gets a copy under its own privacy policy. Android backup and device transfer may also copy app data; these are controlled by your device settings.
- **No Account Required**: You can use all features of Banat without registering an account, providing an email, or logging in.

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Banat does **NOT**:
- Collect or sell your personal or sensitive health data.
- Track your geographic location.
- Access your contacts, call logs, or SMS.
- Share your data with advertising networks or third-party brokers.
- Use analytics or tracking software to build advertising profiles.

---

## 3. Device Permissions

To deliver core app features, Banat requests the following device permissions:
- **Camera and gallery selection**: Banat uses the system camera app and a system content chooser so you can take or select photos. It does not request broad camera or media-library permissions. Selected images are copied to app-specific storage.
- **Notifications**: Optional local reminders around 2:00 PM, enabled in Settings. On supported Android versions, permission is requested only when you enable reminders. You can decline or disable reminders and continue using the app.

---

## 4. Data Control & Deletion

You have 100% ownership and control over your data:
- **Export Data**: You can export your progress reports as a PDF at any time.
- **Modify Data**: You can update or edit your daily entries, weekly targets, and profile goals whenever you choose.
- **Delete Data**: Clearing app storage or uninstalling Banat removes its local database and app-specific photos. It does not remove reports or photos you exported, copies held by receiving apps, or existing system backups. Manage those copies and backups separately in the relevant app or device settings.

---

## 5. Medical Disclaimer

Banat is an informational health, habit, and progress tracking tool designed for fitness enthusiasm. The App's calculations (including BMR, TDEE, and Rate of Change estimates) are **informational estimates** and do not constitute professional medical advice, diagnosis, or clinical treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider before starting any significant calorie deficit, surplus, or physical training program.
